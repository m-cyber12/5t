01 / PROFILE

I am an electrical engineer who treats copper like typography — every trace deliberate, every component earning its place. My work sits where power electronics meet consequence: if it fails, someone remembers.

Trained at ETH Zürich, sharpened across ABB's grid divisions and a decade of independent practice. I take on few engagements — typically four per year — and go deep: from topology studies and magnetics to firmware, EMC qualification and the certification audits that make a design shippable.

Clients include three of Europe's largest utility operators, a satellite constellation startup, and a medtech firm whose devices now regulate heartbeats on four continents.
